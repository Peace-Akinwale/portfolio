## Homepage, in order

1. Name and one line: who the work is for and what they get.
2. Four blocks, one per ability, each with a heading in plain words, two sentences, one artefact at full column width, one link. Writing: the ManyRequests SERP and the editor comment. Building: the Fanout capture. AI tools: Docs Watch's side-by-side or the OKF graph. Thinking: the reading-app notification story or the Aloud decision.
3. The offer: prices, the tool-building line, one button.
4. Footer.

The three questions ("Is this true?", "What's worth making?", "What does done mean?") are Peace's and she liked them on 23 Oct. They can head the thinking block as its three sentences rather than frame the whole page **(Peace)**.
