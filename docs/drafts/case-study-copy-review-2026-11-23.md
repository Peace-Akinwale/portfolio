## ContentDB: proposed paragraph after the Claude quote-context image

In the example above, I asked Claude to find public sources about the problems agency founders faced before moving to retainers. The response identified unpaid follow-up work in Suraj Sodha’s interview and included the passage with its surrounding context. I could then check that interpretation against the recording before using it in an article. That gives me a specific example to investigate and a way to judge whether it supports the point I want to make.

Evidence: original Claude conversation from 7 November, recaptured on 23 November. This describes what the response returned and the verification available to the writer. It does not claim the transcript quotes have been independently verified or that this research was published in a finished article.

To keep the page focused, consolidate the existing repeated benefits in “Key features,” “What makes it different” and “Why it matters” during the next approved prose pass. Do not append more generic speed or credibility claims.

A marketer can open an update, see which part of their work it affects, and compare the original wording before deciding what to change. I included the source comparison so the team can check my interpretation and decide how the change applies to their own work.

Evidence: the current case study describes source versions, reports, comparisons and Slack delivery; the live public app exposes the report and source comparison. This proposes a clearer account of the existing workflow, without claiming an unverified business result.

## Writing checks

- Keep the existing first-person voice and sentence-case headings.
- Add the practical consequence beside the feature or decision it explains.
- Preserve meaningful distinctions, including captured versus simulated search activity and source extraction versus human review.
- No invented adoption figures, time savings, client outcomes or testimonials.
- Leave approved quotations unchanged.
- Screen for repeated points and generic endings before adding length.
