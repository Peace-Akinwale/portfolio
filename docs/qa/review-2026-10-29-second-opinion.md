# Second review, 29 Oct 2026 (another agent, pasted by Peace)

Kept verbatim for the record. Cross-checked the same day: the contact-form stages, the testimonials LinkedIn links and the job-seeking blog post are real; the "blank desktop calendar" is the screenshot tool, Calendly loads in a real browser.

1. Homepage gives the heading the most emphasis; on mobile the opening screen has no work or hiring button outside the menu.
2. The two-column sections read as essays; screenshots feel secondary.
3. Case-study index cards are text-only and look interchangeable; show a screen from each tool.
4. Case-study hero is a large rounded panel with empty space on the right; the product appears late.
5. Index, projects and ManyRequests pages don't feel like one design system; heading sizes and widths vary.
6. Testimonials: long italic text across a wide card; lead with a short upright excerpt.
7. Contact: "Where are you in the process?" options are all about content strategy, so a building client can't answer; give desktop the direct Calendly link too.
8. Portfolio leads with recent posts; lead with selected client work and put the archive below.
9. About repeats the process; the move from writing into building is the interesting story; portrait sits far right with a gap.
10. Absolute promises: "The editor has nothing left to fix", "I check every screen and action".
11. Services: "one round of revisions" beside "we work on it together until it does"; rush delivery priced under "What's not included"; no currency; for tools, say what happens after handover (hosting, support).
12. Two destinations for built things; About's "Explore the tools I've built" points to /projects while the nav points to /case-studies; ManyRequests has an "All the things I built" return link.
Smaller: link the AI Docs Watch examples to their evidence pages; testimonials "View on LinkedIn" goes to the profile, not the recommendation; blog tags and cut-off excerpts; the old "Looking for content manager/strategy roles" post contradicts the services page.
