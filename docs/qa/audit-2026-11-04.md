# Audit of `redesign/seam-dated`, 4 November 2026

Rendered from the local dev build at 1440 and 390 (`scripts/qa/shoot.mjs`, `docs/qa/shots/`), text read through the council's `page-text.py`. Every route answers 200, no console errors, no horizontal scroll, one H1 each. The faults below are judged against the 4 Nov goal: the site has to show how Peace thinks, that she uses AI tools well and understands a project end to end, that she writes well, and that she builds tools that solve marketing problems.

The short verdict: nothing is broken, and nothing persuades. Every page is tidy and every page describes surfaces. The four abilities are never named, never shown side by side, and the case studies read as feature tours because they were written from summaries.

## Site-wide

**Design**
- One flat sky ground (`#D9E4EC`) on every page, cards two shades lighter, marigold on three buttons. The pages look the same at a glance and nothing is allowed to be big.
- Screenshots sit at 520 to 600px wide on a 1440 screen, so the proof (SERP, Docs Watch change page, Fanout capture) is decoration you have to lean into.
- Three repeated closing blocks (navy "Want a tool like this" card, "If you liked this one" pair, four-column footer) end every page the same way.
- The floating "N" circle bottom-left is the Next dev overlay, not the site. Ignore it.

**Copy**
- The mechanical scans are clean (no adverbs, no triplets, no em dashes outside the WordPress AEO post and the career tool). The faults are in what the copy chooses to say: features and process instead of decisions and outcomes.
- "with me" appears in six headings and CTAs (Writing with me, Building with me, Work with me, Want to be next?). Coy, and it repeats.
- Agency anonymity ("an SEO agency's founder and strategists") appears 11 times across the site. It is the one fact every council seat asked for and the site keeps withholding it without saying why.
- No page has a sentence that shows thinking as a distinct ability. "How I work" is the only candidate and it is a process description.

**Page set**
- 23 public routes for a one-person site. Three of them list the same two articles (blog, case-studies index, writing page). Four dead routes sit behind redirects. One route is an unrelated career quiz.

**Type**
- Three families on every screen: Sprat for display, Newsreader for the lede and long-form, DM Sans for the line under the lede, the nav, labels and buttons. The hero alone changes voice three times in 300px.
- Sprat at 90 to 96px: wide, flared, letterspaced. It reads as a fashion or interiors masthead, not a consultant's. The H1 "Fanout / query / analysis / tool" breaks into four lines on desktop because of the width axis.
- Newsreader at 17 to 18px on the sky ground has thin strokes and prints grey. The long-form pages look faded next to the headings.
- Sprat H2s every 300px on the case-study pages make a ten-section page read as ten headlines with captions under them.

## Per page

### Home (`/`), 4,741px at 1440, 8,420px at 390
- Design: the navy half under "Building" runs empty for about 300px under the Fanout block; the three question sections use a quote rule for Peace's own answer, so her claim looks like someone else's pull quote; the portrait is the largest object on the page and says nothing about the work.
- Copy: the H1 names no reader and no offer; the line under it says what but not the outcome; the third line is a list of nouns without a verb; three layers of framing (Accuracy / "Is this true?" / the answer) before the first proof; the Fanout stat "3 AI tools" is a feature count; the Q3 answer makes two absolute promises ("The editor has nothing left to fix"); "How I work" is where the thinking ability should live and it describes a checklist.
- Page set: repeats the case-study blurbs and the services block word for word.
- Type: as site-wide. The question nav (DM Sans 15px) disappears between the Sprat blocks.

### Services (`/services`), 3,402px
- Design: the FAQ is thirteen questions in two columns, 1,700px of grey; the Building half has no proof in it (no quote, no screenshot, no link to a study inside the half); nothing on the page shows a result.

### Case studies index (`/case-studies`), 3,649px
