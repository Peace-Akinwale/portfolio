## Agreed changes, ranked

1. **Cut "Old Essays + a thing or two from my journalism days"** (all three). The section and "Satire: The commandments for men" end the page before the call card. The CMO, as devil's advocate, argued the essays show she can hold an opinion, and was not convinced: that belongs in one line on About. The section comes from WordPress; Peace removes it there or the page filters it out. (Peace)
2. **Point the header "Book a call" at the booking page** (all three). It opens /contact while "Book a 30-minute call" opens Calendly.
3. **Name the Marker.io head of marketing and move his words up beside the Jabra comment** (all three). "Their head of marketing wrote that they never needed more than one round of feedback per article." is the editor-hours proof both buyers want. Show it as the document, named, in the top strip. (Peace)
4. **One heading form, and titles that look like links** (agency founder, design critic). "Published B2B SaaS content for ManyRequests:" and "For pangea.ai, an HRTech SaaS platform for tech start-ups to hire developers in 24 hours." follow different patterns. Rename "More from this client" to what it opens, or cut it. Replace "Toggl vs Clockify: comparison article to show ManyRequests as an alternative" with the published title. Most headings are WordPress content. (Peace)
5. **Add a dated demo-requests line from Regine Garcia as its own quote** (CMO). Not stacked under the April screenshot, where it would read as a hedge. (Peace)
6. **Agency volume in words** (agency founder). Add volume and turnaround to "Published under the client’s bylines." and one agency clause after "Tell me about the product and the readers you want to reach." Put the Exceed SEO lead's comment beside the Jabra one. (Peace)
7. **Crop the ranking screenshot for 390** (design critic).

## Disagreements

- **Agency proof inside the client list.** The agency founder wanted a quote and figures inside the two Exceed SEO groups. The design critic argued that breaks the one shape the list works by, and the 26 titles already prove volume. The founder accepted: the proof goes in the top strip and in words.
- **A client outcome under the ranking screenshot.** The CMO wanted a demos line under the April image. The agency founder argued a demos claim stacked on a lapsed ranking reads as a hedge. The CMO moved it to its own quote.
- **Link styling as a booking factor.** The CMO called it hygiene. The design critic and the founder kept it in their lists. Chair's call: do it; it costs minutes.
