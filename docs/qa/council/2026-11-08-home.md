# Council verdict: homepage (V2 with client logos), 8 November 2026

**Decision put to the council:** Would you book the call from this page?
**Target:** http://localhost:3000/ (dev server, working tree equal to commit `2502e8f` on `design/v2-logos`, the compact V2 homepage) rendered at 18:15 WAT (253 body words). Rendered text, screenshots at 1440 and 390, and every round: session scratchpad `council/home-v2/`.
**Seats:** CMO, agency founder, Head of Content and SEO, design critic, copy editor. Chair: Claude, running `~/.claude/skills/hiring-council`.
**Previous verdict for this page:** `docs/qa/council/2026-11-04-home.md` (6 to 7 across five seats, on the longer 4 Nov homepage).

## The verdict in one paragraph

Three of five would book, all narrowly, and neither buyer who matters most for volume is among them: the agency founder and the Head of Content would email first. The shorter page works. Every seat named Regine Garcia's quote, "His top-performing posts are product-led ones, which has led to more traffic and demo requests for ManyRequests.", as the strongest thing on the first screen, and the logo strip gave the agency founder the best white-label signal the site has shown, in "via Spicy Margarita" under the Jabra mark. What holds the page back is that its proof has to be assembled by the reader. The quote has no figure, the only dated ranking lives in image alt text, two of the four "Selected work" cards ("Record AI search activity so strategists can analyze citations, brand mentions and why AI platforms choose a client’s competitors." and "Find useful quotes and their original context in your saved research while writing in Claude or ChatGPT.") describe software with no maker, user or date, and nothing in the words tells an agency there is a door for it. The fixes the council agreed on are small and mostly fit inside lines the page already has, which keeps the compact page Peace chose.

## Agreed changes, ranked

1. **Put the ranking under the SERP screenshot as a visible, dated caption** (CMO, Head of Content, design critic, copy editor). Evidence: the image alt "Google results for “design annotation tools” in April 2026, with the ManyRequests article in third place, marked with an arrow" is the only dated result on the page, and no reader sees it. One caption line, taken from the alt, keeps the month. The 4 Nov verdict found the article off page one on 3 Nov, so the caption must say April 2026, or be replaced by a current reading. (Peace)
2. **Make every card link say "Read the case study"** (design critic, Head of Content, agency founder). Evidence: "Read a bit about my craft ↗" and "Explore ContentDB ↗" sit beside two cards that say "Read the case study ↗" and all four go to case studies. The craft link is the one line the agency founder would have to explain to a client. Approved copy, so Peace's call. (Peace)
3. **Rewrite the closing line, and add the agency door to it** (copy editor, agency founder). Evidence: "Tell me about your product, the article you need, or the task your team keeps repeating." lists three things and speaks only to an in-house buyer. The copy editor's fix: "Tell me about the article you need or the task your team keeps repeating." The agency founder adds two sentences after it once Peace supplies the numbers, in the copy editor's draft: "Agencies, I also write under your name for your clients, up to [N] articles a month. A draft reaches you [N] working days after the brief, with [N] rounds of edits included." The rounds figure must match Services ("One round of revisions."). The same closing line sits in `content/pages/about.md`; change both. (Peace)
4. **Move Nathan Vander Heyden's one-round line onto the homepage under Regine's quote** (CMO, agency founder). Evidence: "Marker.io" is only a logo here, while the Services and testimonials pages already publish his words "we never needed more than one round of feedback per article" (quoted from those pages, not this one). Editor hours are the hidden cost both buyers price in; the proof exists and is already public.
5. **Rewrite the Exceed SEO card in the same voice as its neighbour** (copy editor, agency founder). Evidence: "Comparison content, listicles and practical how-to guides for two event software clients." lists three things, has no number and does not say whose clients they are. Copy editor's version: "I've written comparison content and how-to guides for two of Exceed SEO's event software clients." Add the article count. The line is the `blurb` in `content/case-studies/exceed-seo.md`, so the case-study index changes with it. (Peace)

Single-seat changes the chair endorses, in order: recapture the Lateef Slack image in light theme without the hover toolbar and recrop the fanout and ContentDB images to one readable panel each (design critic); name Peace as the builder and give the users on the two tool cards (Head of Content, copy editor in round 1); give BOFU, MOFU and fanout a plain word on first use (copy editor); a "from" figure in "See services and prices ↗" (CMO).

## Disagreements

- **Name AEO in the intro.** The Head of Content wanted "AEO, citation tracking, a content pipeline" in the hero because "build workflows to automate marketing tasks" reads as automation freelancing. The CMO attacked: the page shows no prompt set, citation rate or date, so the label claims an outcome before the proof. The design critic and copy editor added that it would be a fourth undefined acronym on the first screen. The Head of Content withdrew it in round 3. Chair's call: no AEO in the hero until a dated reading is on the page; the title and meta can carry the search term.
- **Logos in one ink.** The design critic wants all eight in one ink with no boxes, because eight brand colours outshout the H1 on the first screen. The agency founder conceded recognition survives (six of eight are wordmarks) but held that "via Spicy Margarita" must stay directly under Jabra; the critic accepted. Chair's call: open. The coloured strip is part of the V2 Peace said they like, and the evidence is a taste rule, not a buyer objection. Peace decides. (Peace)
- **Agency terms on the homepage or on Services.** The design critic, as devil's advocate, put all agency terms on Services. The agency founder kept two sentences on the homepage because the page must say the door exists. Chair's call: the two sentences replace the closing line rather than add a section, and only once Peace has the real numbers; full terms stay on Services.

## Devil's advocate

Every buyer seat asked for more words: a ranking line, retainer length, a price, an agency line, tool usage lines, AEO. The design critic argued against all of it: the additions come to roughly 90 to 100 words on a 253-word page Peace cut down because both the longer homepage and an earlier V2 "still felt text-heavy" (Peace's earlier review, per the project handoff), and most belong one click away. It was convinced only on the ranking caption. It moved the others. The CMO dropped a separate price line for a figure inside the existing link and folded the retainer year into the existing card line; the agency founder moved full terms to Services; the Head of Content dropped AEO. Net result: the agreed changes add about 40 words, most of them inside lines that already exist.

## Method and limits

- Rounds: independent positions, debate (each seat attacked one other's weakest point; devil's advocate on the call for more words), vote.
- Verified by the chair against the render: title 55 characters, meta description 152 characters, one H1, JSON-LD Person and WebSite, 14 descriptive alts, every card image link has an aria-label in source, the header "Let’s chat" and body booking buttons both go to Calendly (the 4 Nov two-destination fault is fixed), no horizontal overflow at 1440 or 390. Nathan's one-round words verified on the Services and testimonials pages. Every quote in the seats' answers was checked against the rendered text; none was struck.
- Struck by the chair: a CMO item asking for a pronoun correction in Regine's quote. The testimonial's wording is accurate; the error was in the seat's own notes.
- Inferred, not verified: the article's current position for "design annotation tools" (the 3 Nov check is the latest the council has).
- Not checked: dark mode, the live Calendly page, the production build (this was the dev server), and any page but the homepage.
- Peace may still be editing the page; this verdict describes the render at the time stated above.
