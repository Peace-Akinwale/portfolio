# Council verdict: services, 4 November 2026

**Decision put to the council:** Would you book the call from this page?
**Target:** http://localhost:3000/services (the production build of `redesign/seam-dated` after the 4 Nov rebuild) rendered at 16:56 WAT. Rendered text, screenshots and every round: session scratchpad `council/services/`.
**Seats:** CMO, agency founder, Head of Content and SEO, design critic, copy editor. Chair: Claude, running `~/.claude/skills/hiring-council`.
**Previous verdict for this page:** none.

## The verdict in one paragraph

Three of five seats would book; the CMO and the Head of Content would email first. The prices carry the page: "Five articles a month, $2,700", the revision rule and the agency line under "Writing" earned the agency founder's 7 and a trial brief. Two things hold the page back. First, the one outcome a CMO can repeat has lapsed with no caveat. The caption reads "One of 51 articles for ManyRequests, on page one of Google for “design annotation tools” in April 2026.", and on 3 Nov the article was off page one. Second, the page contradicts itself on turnaround: "3 to 5 business days from brief to first draft." sits beside "48 to 72 hours each". The header "Book a call" goes to /contact while every body button goes to Calendly. Tool building and strategy have no price, so the Head of Content cannot book either offer.

## Agreed changes, ranked

1. **Point the header "Book a call" at the Calendly link** (CMO, Head of Content, design critic; the agency founder conceded it in debate). Every "Book a 30-minute call" opens Calendly, and the header opens the form. Under "Next step", relabel "Contact" as Email me and point it at the mailto (design critic, from the copy editor).
2. **Make the turnaround figures agree** (agency founder, Head of Content, copy editor's facts). Under "Before you book": "3 to 5 business days from brief to first draft." against "48 to 72 hours each" and "Rush delivery under 2 business days, $700 per article". Say what each figure measures. (Peace)
3. **Fix the first screen** (copy editor, design critic). Swap "BOFU" for "bottom-of-funnel" in "I write BOFU content for B2B SaaS companies and agencies". Replace the colon-led "Clients so far:" with a sentence, then the names. Keep the wayfinding sentence, which holds the only first-screen links to the samples and the case studies. (Peace) because the BOFU line is her approved wording.
4. **Fix the ranking caption, then re-shoot both proof images** (CMO, design critic). Keep the April date and say plainly it is a dated reading. Add where the article stands now, or a second query that holds today. Then put Regine Garcia's attributed line on demo requests beside it, with no unsourced pipeline figure. Re-shoot both images to one frame, cropped to what the caption names and readable at 390. (Peace)
5. **Put numbers on the tool and strategy offers** (Head of Content). "Custom pricing, set once we’ve talked about the task." and "Scoped per engagement." give a buyer nothing to budget. Add a build range, a retainer fee and a strategy starting price, under the existing headings and not as a new H2 (the CMO's point). (Peace)
6. **Move the agency terms out of the FAQ** (agency founder). Put "For agencies, 4 to 6 articles a month at 48 to 72 hours each, from your brief and your client’s product, in your editor’s house style." under "Writing" as a paragraph, say white-label, and extend "Three or more new articles at once also get 10% off." to agency volume rather than adding price rows. Add a dated next-opening line. (Peace)
7. **The copy pass** (copy editor, CMO). Split the three-item sentences. Rename "Original research", which reads as first-party data. Say whether "Five articles a month, $2,700" means new articles. Put a named client beside "which most clients stop needing by month two" or cut the clause. Make Peace the subject of "Every tool keeps a decisions file".

## Disagreements

- **A third H2 for strategy.** The Head of Content wanted strategy priced under its own heading. The CMO argued a third offer makes a buyer choose before saying yes to articles. The Head of Content accepted. Chair's call: the price goes under "Writing".
- **Agency price rows.** The agency founder wanted prices at 4 and 6 articles a month. The Head of Content showed the 10% rule already prices them, and a fourth price row would clutter the block. The founder accepted. Chair's call: say the rule in words for agencies.
- **"BOFU" in brackets or swapped.** The copy editor bracketed the term; the design critic showed the bracket pushes the first price off the 390 first screen. The copy editor conceded. Chair's call: swap the word.
