## Disagreements

- **Where the price goes.** The CMO wanted a build range on this page. The Head of Content argued a price belongs in one place on the site, or the copies drift. Chair's call: one sentence here, the range on /services.
- **Opening with the miss.** The Head of Content wanted the 152-passage miss in the opening paragraph. The agency founder argued no buyer forwards a page that opens on the writer's mistake. Chair's call: open on the four weeks' warning; the miss follows as a clause.
- **Whose words the quotes are.** The agency founder suspected the quotes under "What I decided, and why" were the founder's. The chair confirmed they are Peace's own words from the build sessions. The founder withdrew and asked for one framing line.

## Devil's advocate

The Head of Content argued against the demand for a reader count: a monitor proves itself by what it catches, and four dated catches are public. Partly convinced. The strip should change now without a count, but readers are reach, not dependence, so a count would still move the score to 8.
