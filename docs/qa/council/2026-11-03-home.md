# Council verdict: homepage, 3 November 2026

**Decision put to the council:** Would you book the call from this page?
**Target:** http://localhost:3000/ rendered at 20:44 WAT, the build after the structure pass (nav Services, Case studies, Writing, Blog, About me, Say hi; the reading app in the "done" slot; the buyer-first lines under the heading). Rendered text and screenshots: session scratchpad `council/home/`.
**Seats:** CMO, agency founder, Head of Content and SEO, design critic, copy editor. Chair: Claude, running `~/.claude/skills/hiring-council`.
**Previous verdict for this page:** none. The 3 Nov hire-me plan (`docs/plans/2026-11-03-hire-me-plan.md`) was a manual first run with three seats on the previous build.
**Rounds run:** independent positions and the debate. The vote was taken by the chair from each seat's revised round-2 score, because the chair's sub-session was stopped before round 3; the per-seat sections say where a score moved and why.

## The verdict in one paragraph

Nobody books outright, and the CMO would not book at all. The proof that holds every seat is the same four lines: "51 articles for ManyRequests since 2024", the dated ranking with "Rankings move, so this is a dated screenshot, not a promise.", the Fanout catch "6 of 10 citations the first parser found on one capture. I checked the raw stream and fixed it before the client saw the number", and "Five articles a month, $2,700". What holds all five back is that the page proves judgment and never proves use or outcome: the tools belong to "an SEO agency's strategists" with no name, no count and no date; the one buyer who can price the work (writing) had no call to book; and a sentence in the proof slot under "What does done mean?" did not parse, on the homepage of someone selling the last pass. The CMO's one line: "I am buying proofreading."

## Agreed changes, ranked

1. **Give the writing buyer a call to book** (five seats). Evidence: under "Writing with me" the buttons were "Email about an article" and "What you get"; the only "Book a 30-minute call" sat under "Building with me" beside "No price list for this one." The header "Book a call" goes to /contact. The CMO, the founder and the Head of Content all called it the wrong door for the buyer who can approve "$2,700" without a call. *Fixed after this render: the writing offer now has "Book a 30-minute call" first.*
2. **Fix the sentence that does not parse** (five seats, after debate). Evidence, under "Done is measured on the live app, not reported by a test.": "The first notification this app sent said a post was in the feed and the week's roundup. It wasn't, and that became the first lesson in the article." Every seat read it twice; the CMO dropped a point for it. *Fixed after this render: "said a post was in the feed and in the week's roundup. The roundup didn't have it."*
3. **One tool, one name** (four seats). Evidence: the hero says "A search recorder and a page audit tool an SEO agency's strategists use.", the meta says "a search recorder for AI answers", the section label says "Building Fanout query analysis tool", and the page audit tool is never shown. The design critic: a label that needs a definition is not a name. Chair's call: the hero names the tools (Fanout, Quality Rater) once, and the label keeps the product name.
4. **Say who uses the tools, not just that someone does** (four seats; the design critic dissents on counts). Evidence: "an SEO agency's strategists use" with no name, count or start date. The founder, CMO and Head of Content want a strategist count and a start month beside each tool; the design critic argued a count rebuilds the stat strip Peace removed on 28 Oct and that small honest numbers read as pilots. Chair's call: one sentence of real use per tool ("N strategists at [agency] since [month]") on the case studies, and the agency's name on the homepage if Peace allows it. **(Peace)**
5. **Drop the absolute promises** (three seats). Evidence, under "What does done mean?": "The editor has nothing left to fix, and the app finishes the job and tells you what happened." and under "Is this true?": "I check every screen and action so it works as it should." The founder: a client holds her to the zero on the first revision round. These are the draft answers marked "for Peace to approve" in `home-copy.ts`. **(Peace)**
6. **Translate the jargon on first use** (three seats). Evidence: "I write BOFU content for B2B SaaS companies and agencies" under "Writing with me"; "AEO" and "Fanout" appear with no definition. The design critic's rule: the bracket goes in the sentence, never in a heading or label.
7. **Cut the "3"** (CMO and design critic agree; keep "6 of 10"). Evidence, the stat block under "Research before code, then analysis as the output.": "3 AI tools it records in: ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity" is a feature count; "6 of 10" is the one line that shows where her check overruled the machine. The CMO: "Proof I would repeat upstairs should be the largest thing in its card, not the smallest." The block is in Peace's approved brief passage, so the cut is hers. **(Peace)**
8. **The title reads as if she were the tools** (copy editor, Head of Content). Evidence: "Peace Akinwale, B2B SaaS content writer and AI tools for content teams". *Fixed after this render: "Peace Akinwale, B2B SaaS content writer and AI tool builder".*
9. **One line for agencies under "Writing with me"** (founder; Head of Content agrees on one line only). The founder wanted turnaround, open slots, white-label and overlap hours; the Head of Content showed that belongs on /services, which the block already links to. Chair's call: one sentence here (white-label yes or no, brief-to-draft turnaround), the rest on Services. **(Peace)**
10. **The hero closed on meta-commentary** (copy editor, CMO). Evidence: "The three questions below are how I decide what's worth making and whether it's true." *Fixed after this render: cut.*

## Disagreements

- **A user count on the homepage.** Three buyer seats want numbers; the design critic (Peace's taste) says counts rebuild the pattern she removed and small numbers read as pilots. The debate moved the critic to "yes on replacing the anonymous line with one sentence of real use, no on counts". Chair's call: the sentence on the homepage, the count on the case study. Open until Peace supplies the numbers.
- **Demote "6 of 10" for a screenshot.** The design critic proposed it; the CMO argued the stat is the only judgment proof in the building column and three seats picked it independently. The critic conceded in part (cut the "3", keep the stat). Chair's call: keep "6 of 10" as the one big figure.
- **Rewrite the Docs Watch paragraph to lead with the 152-passage story.** The Head of Content wanted it; the copy editor showed the paragraph is Peace's approved brief wording and no seat rewrites it. Chair's call: the story goes in the caption or the H3, both unapproved text, with Peace's wording. **(Peace)**
- **Agency terms on the homepage.** Founder for, Head of Content against. Resolved to one line here, the rest on Services.

## Devil's advocate

The design critic argued against the change every buyer seat agreed on (a user count beside every tool). It moved the founder and the CMO to accept "one sentence of real use" as the homepage form, with the count on the case study. It did not move anyone off wanting the count somewhere.
