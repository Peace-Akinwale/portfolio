## The verdict in one paragraph

No seat would book from this page. Scores fell in debate to 3 to 4. The page carries a false product line, written in this rebuild: "with a chat that can bring a chosen source into the question" under ContentDB describes a web chat removed on 31 Oct. The OKF card misleads too: "nothing marked verified until a person has checked it against the original" hides that a scripted check gave 381 cards a label the app shows like human review. Every tool card ends in a status, not an outcome: "An SEO agency’s founder and team, since June 2026" says who and when, never how many or what changed. The Fanout card, "Built for an SEO agency’s strategists, August to September 2026", reads as a tool nobody uses now, because it never says what the run left behind. The writing work, the thing a buyer pays for, gets one line at the bottom, and the page never asks for the call. The header "Book a call" is its only booking action.

## Agreed changes, ranked

The accuracy fix ranks third by the count of seats, but it takes minutes and the page is wrong until it lands. Do it first.

1. **Turn every status line into an outcome line** (all three). "An SEO agency’s founder and team, since June 2026", "Built for an SEO agency’s strategists, August to September 2026" and "An SEO agency’s strategists, first version, since October 2026" become people, frequency and what the tool replaced. Fanout keeps its true end date and adds what the run produced. (Peace)
2. **Give Writing a full row with dated proof, then end on a call card** (all three). "51 product-led articles for ManyRequests since 2024, with the search results they earned." is two lines of link text under the tools. Give it a row equal to the tools, with one dated result and a client's words, then the two-button "Book a 30-minute call" card. The CMO wanted it above the tools; the agency founder and the design critic accept a full row. (Peace)
3. **Check every card against its build record** (agency founder, CMO; the design critic listed it as a Peace item). Fix "with a chat that can bring a chosen source into the question". Reword "nothing marked verified until a person has checked it against the original". Cut or finish "Also built: Reach, hand the step to a local session on the right Mac, pause for passwords, and send the result back." Define OKF on first use.
4. **Re-shoot the covers under one capture rule and give each an alt** (design critic). Light theme, nothing cut mid-element, Aloud in 4:5. Six of eight covers break the rule. The images carry empty alts today.

## Disagreements

- **The Fanout end date.** The agency founder read "August to September 2026" as a dead tool. The design critic argued finished work is normal in a practitioner's record and the missing reason is the defect. The CMO and the founder accepted. Chair's call: keep the dates and say what the run left behind.
- **Cards or the one-column list.** The design critic called the card grid a rejected pattern and noted it replaced the 29 Oct dated list without a logged decision from Peace. The CMO argued the layout gives a CEO no new sentence and outranks nothing. Chair's call: open. The layout is Peace's decision; the outcome lines come first either way. (Peace)
- **Writing on this page at all.** The agency founder, as devil's advocate, argued the page names its own job and the nav already has Writing. Partly convinced: the ManyRequests case study is a case study, so it earns a row, not the top of the page.

## Devil's advocate
