**Target:** http://localhost:3000/ (the production build of `redesign/seam-dated` after the 4 Nov rebuild) rendered at 16:56 WAT. Rendered text, screenshots and every round: session scratchpad `council/home/`.

## The verdict in one paragraph

Three of five seats would book, narrowly; the CMO would email first. The rebuild moved the buyers. The hero now says what she does and for whom, and the agency founder and Head of Content both called the catches the strongest senior evidence on the site: "On one capture, the teammate’s bookmarklet reported 6 citations. The answer carried 10." and the AI Docs Watch rewrite story ending in "Every report now lists the new headings first." What holds every seat back is the same set of things the old page had, plus one new fact. The tools still have no head count ("Tools an SEO agency’s team has used since June 2026."). The one writing outcome has lapsed: the chair's check found "design annotation tools" off page one on 3 Nov, so the H2 "A comparison article that put the client’s product on page one." reads as current when it is not, and the screenshot that proves the April position is unreadable on a phone. And the header "Book a call" goes to /contact while every body button goes to Calendly. No seat reached 8.

## Agreed changes, ranked

1. **Point the header "Book a call" at the same Calendly link as every "Book a 30-minute call"** (five seats). Evidence: the link list shows "Book a call -> /contact" in the header and Calendly on every body button. Two destinations for the one action the council judges. Minutes of work.
2. **Fix the lapsed ranking in the copy, and make the screenshot readable on a phone** (CMO, Head of Content, design critic, agency founder). Evidence: "By April 2026 it sat on page one of Google for “design annotation tools”." and the H2 "A comparison article that put the client’s product on page one." The April screenshot shows the article third in organic results; on 3 Nov it was off page one. Say "third organic result, April 2026" in the body, keep "Rankings move, so this is a dated screenshot, not a promise.", and crop the phone image to the ManyRequests row. A second query that holds today, or current Search Console data, replaces the lapsed proof. (Peace)
3. **Put a number on the tools** (CMO, agency founder, Head of Content). Evidence: "Tools an SEO agency’s team has used since June 2026." and "Built for an SEO agency’s strategists in August and September 2026." A strategist count, agency size and one dated citation reading turn the second half of the H1, "Tools your content team uses every week.", into something a buyer can repeat. (Peace)
4. **Answer the editor-hours question under the prices** (CMO, agency founder, design critic). Evidence: "Five articles a month, $2,700" sits alone; "What every article includes" is the only link out. One plain sentence with Nathan Vander Heyden's one-round line, named, plus turnaround; relabel the link "Turnaround and agency terms", because /services already states both. No box, no extra rows (design critic). (Peace) for permission to name him.
5. **Recapture the proof screenshots** (design critic). Evidence: the Docs Watch image ("A change on Google’s documentation, before and after, on the public site.") is the only dark capture and is cut mid-sentence; the reading-app caption describes a notification the screenshot does not show. Light theme, cut at an element edge, captions that say what is on screen.
6. **The mechanical copy pass** (copy editor, agency founder). Split "The rewrite had 152 changed passages, the report step saw the first 24, and the section that mattered began at 51." and the 6-of-10 sentence, naming the fanout query analysis tool as the extension; split "A passing test is not done; a real person on the live app is."; drop the colon in "One recorded answer: the brands it named, the pages it cited."; give AEO a plain word in "follow SEO and AEO trends"; trim the 192-character meta description; say whose "the teammate’s bookmarklet" is.

## Disagreements

- **"content engineer" and "AEO" in the title.** The Head of Content wanted the job title and AEO in the title and the Writing block. The CMO attacked: a budget holder searches for articles that bring demos, and labelling the offer AEO before the page shows a citation reading claims an outcome not yet shown. The Head of Content conceded the order. Chair's call: earn the word first. AEO enters the title after a dated reading is on the page.
- **Cut or keep "AI Docs Watch, public, checked every day."** The design critic called it a status. The Head of Content argued it is the only hero proof a buyer can check without an NDA and should be swapped for usage, not emptied. The design critic changed position in round 3. Chair's call: rewrite it to say what it watches ("AI Docs Watch checks AI platforms’ docs every day, in public." was the copy editor's version) and add a count when one exists.
- **Two failure stories as a pattern.** The CMO read "the monitor caught it and reported the wrong part" and "The roundup didn’t have it." as two failures on one homepage. The agency founder showed the Docs Watch story ends in a fix and the third story is someone else's tool caught by her. The CMO withdrew the charge in round 3. Open item: the reading-app caption should say what changed.
