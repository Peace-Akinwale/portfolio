# How I wrote 51 product-led articles for ManyRequests and made the product useful inside the piece

ManyRequests sells client portal and agency management software. Its buyers are comparing tools, working out how to manage client requests, and looking for better ways to run their agencies.

I’ve written 51 articles for ManyRequests since 2024. The work includes comparisons and alternatives pages, alongside guides to agency pricing, retainers and client delivery.

## Choosing topics the product could help explain

I started with what the reader needed to do, then looked for the parts I could explain through ManyRequests. Someone researching a retainer needs to understand how to run one, including how to collect requests and charge for work outside the agreed scope. I can use the product to show how the agency would collect that request or sell the additional service.

That made software comparisons and operational problems useful places to start. Someone searching for a client-facing project management tool might need to collect requests, get approvals and handle billing. Those are concrete questions I could answer through the product.

I used business potential to judge those opportunities. When the connection was weak, I changed the angle or treated the piece as educational content. The article still had to answer the reader’s question.
