For an article, you can onboard me through your process and share the topic or brief with me. I'll also need to know the target reader and access to the product. If access isn’t possible, I’ll need someone who can answer product questions and links to your help pages to find how the product genuinely works. I'll also need to know the limitations, so anything from sales calls will help. For an AI automation, just tell me what you need.

A first draft usually takes three to five business days after I have the brief and access to your product. For agency retainers, I can work to an agreed schedule of four to six articles a month, depending on your needs.
