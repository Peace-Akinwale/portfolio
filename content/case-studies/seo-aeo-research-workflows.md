I built tools for Steve Toth that bring relevant SEO, AEO, and marketing posts into one reading workspace and track changes to AI platform documentation.

## One place for industry reading

The reading workspace brings together relevant content from LinkedIn, X, YouTube, and blogs. Steve can read, save useful posts and decide what to respond to without repeatedly opening separate feeds throughout the day.

## See what changed in AI documentation

The documentation monitor tracks changes to AI platform docs so the team can review what changed and consider how it affects, or could improve, its work as an AEO-first agency.

The team can compare the original wording before deciding whether its content, research or workflows need to change. [See how the documentation monitor works](/case-studies/ai-docs-watch).

## My contribution

I built the reading and documentation-monitoring tools around the team's need to follow relevant updates and act on them. Bringing the posts together reduces repeated checking across separate sources; keeping the documentation comparisons gives the team a way to check what changed.
