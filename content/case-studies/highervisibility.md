<h2 id="traffic-evidence">From 14,000 to 35,000 monthly organic visits</h2><p>My content helped build that growth. I covered the questions HigherVisibility’s prospective clients were asking: when to hire an SEO consultant, what SEO should cost, how to measure progress, and how to turn website visitors into customers.</p><figure class="evidence"><a href="/case-studies/highervisibility/traffic.png"><img src="/case-studies/highervisibility/traffic.png" width="1642" height="916" alt="Historical Semrush chart for highervisibility.com, with organic traffic rising between July 2023 and January 2024"></a></figure>
<h2>Research that gave the articles something to say</h2><p>I included insights from practitioners, real product examples, and HigherVisibility’s client work in the articles. Early in the engagement, I proposed sourcing expert insights from LinkedIn, X, and industry publications. I also asked Adam for case studies with the results and the process/steps they took to achieve those results, so I could show readers how the agency solved the problems I was writing about.</p><p>That approach helped me understand the problems the readers have, explain the said problem, show what the solution looks like in practice, and how the reader can DIY the solution too.</p>
<h2>See it in the writing</h2><p>These examples span the engagement, from SEO buying advice to lead generation and ecommerce conversion.</p>
<section class="writing-example"><h3><a href="https://www.highervisibility.com/seo/learn/why-seo-expensive/">Why Is SEO So Expensive? ↗</a></h3><p>I unpacked the costs behind the service: competition, tools, specialist work, and staffing. Practitioner perspectives made the budget discussion concrete. The article gives a buyer a way to assess what they’re paying for and which kind of help they need.</p></section>
<section class="writing-example"><h3><a href="https://www.highervisibility.com/website-design/learn/ways-generate-more-leads-website/">15 Ways to Generate More Leads on Your Website ↗</a></h3><p>I examined Zapier’s lead forms, Mutiny’s lead magnet, Namecheap’s live chat, and Semrush’s scroll popup. I explained how the details work: which questions qualify a prospect, how support removes friction, and why an offer fits the page a visitor is reading. Readers can see what to adapt for their own websites.</p></section>
<section class="writing-example"><h3><a href="https://www.highervisibility.com/seo/learn/user-personas-for-seo-how-they-can-lead-to-more-growth/">User Personas for SEO ↗</a></h3><p>I used Tas Bober’s discussion of product language and Sendoso’s positioning to explain a costly disconnect: companies and customers can describe the same solution differently. Then I showed how interviews, surveys, and research analysis can inform the language a business uses to attract buyers.</p></section>
<section class="writing-example"><h3><a href="https://www.highervisibility.com/website-design/learn/how-to-increase-online-sales/">How to Increase Online Sales Fast ↗</a></h3><p>I interpreted a Hotjar recording in which a shopper repeatedly clicked a button before noticing the size selector, then suggested making the controls clearer. Alongside landing-page examples and checkout fixes, it shows readers how to spot the specific obstacles between a visit and a sale.</p></section>
